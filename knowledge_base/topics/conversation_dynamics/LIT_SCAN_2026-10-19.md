## Signal: the lane exploded in Sep–Oct 2026

Full-duplex turn-taking went from niche to a crowded subfield in ~6 weeks. Grouped:

**Endpoointing / semantic VAD (our core lane):**
- 2609.35791 FD-VAD (UMD, 09-16): semantic endpoint detection for streaming full-duplex speech —
  pause = hesitation vs completed intent. Directly the semantic-VAD thesis.
- 2610.21150 VoxTurn (Melbourne, 10-15, 8 votes): one model answers several turn-taking questions
  in one pass, for *cascaded* agents. Closest to our cascade framing.
- 2609.34096 HiThink Turn (09-28): intent-aware control module — end-of-turn prediction alone is
  not enough (complete utterances may need no response).
- 2610.12802 People Make Conversation (10-08): evaluates the *decision at every pause*, not
  boundary detection aggregates. Same critique we make of binary endpointing metrics.

**Evaluation of turn-taking (feeds our instruments/eval products):**
- 2610.08125 Two-Body Problem (KAIST, 10-06, 12 votes): dyadic evaluation of FD models.
- 2609.27372 Intent-Conditioned Evaluation (09-23): binary fixed-window rules are the wrong metric.
- 2610.20172 MultiPartyTurnBench (10-14): floor control beyond dyadic.
- 2609.31967 IndicFDB (NVIDIA, 09-25): Full-Duplex-Bench multilingual extension.

**Cascaded agent architecture (our orchestrator lane):**
- 2609.20995 Voice-Light (09-17, 7 votes): full-duplex *cascaded* agent, causal turn-taking +
  speculative generation. Overlap without canceling on every acknowledgment — exactly the
  event-driven-orchestrator pitch.
- 2609.34247 SALMONN-duo (09-28): dual-system coordination, tools + deliberation inside FD agents.
- 2610.17124 D2 (MIT, 10-12): FD-SLMs from half-duplex backbones.
- 2610.17780 Diffuplex (NVIDIA, 10-13): discrete-diffusion FD-SLM.

**Overlap as third action:** 2609.13117 Continue/Adapt/Yield (09-11) — binary keep-speaking/stop
cannot express "continue while incorporating the listener".

**Ecosystem:** 2610.11299 DuplexAgent-RSI (10-08, 21 votes — hottest): FD model on the live channel
+ heavy work off-channel = the cascade↔duplex bridge pattern our orchestrator paper argues.

## Verdict

1. **Gap check (cascaded latency floor + orchestrator runtime contract):** no paper attacks the

   contract). Gap INTACT for the Nov paper — and now has 4+ citable neighbors proving the lane matters.

   extracting trajectory/coverage from observability traces. Gap INTACT.

   benchmark-adjacent citations for the decision-model lane. Candidate KB cross-refs.
