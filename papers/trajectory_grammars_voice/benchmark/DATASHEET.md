# Datasheet — Synthetic Spanish Voice-Agent Trajectory Benchmark (`tgv-bench/1.0`)

For: *Turn-Level Trajectory Grammars for Voice Agents* (papers/trajectory_grammars_voice, §5.4)
License: **CC-BY-4.0** · Author: Daniel Garcia Pena — info@iamdgarcia.com — ORCID 0000-0002-1456-4785

## Motivation
- **Purpose:** re-run end-to-end the paper's evaluation protocol, baselines, and stopping rule without private production logs.
- **Not for:** training production fraud models, assessing individuals, or claiming real-bank performance figures.

## Composition
- 12,000 synthetic Spanish calls; 5 campaigns × 2 collection cycles; 5 scenario classes (card_block, transfer, balance, dispute, auth_reset).
- Instance: one JSONL record per call — call_id, campaign, cycle, scenario_class, terminal_label (4 classes), cycle_depth D, turns[] with per-turn state/event/text/t_offset/endpoint+bargein flags/tool_rtt_ms.
- Terminal distribution (seed 20261013): contained 8655 / abandoned 1425 / handoff 1316 / compliance-escape 604.
- Text: template replay over Spanish fragments; **no real caller data** — templates are authored, distributions (turn gaps, tool RTT) are parametric Gaussians, not fitted copies.
- Known gaps vs. real logs: no accent/dialect variation, no code-switching, no ASR-noise channel, template lexical diversity is bounded, class weights are deployment-informed priors not measured rates.

## Collection process
Fully synthetic; generated by `generate.py --seed 20261013` (numpy-free stdlib RNG). Deterministic: same seed ⇒ byte-identical corpus.

## Preprocessing / cleaning
None required; schema-validated at generation (event classes restricted to Table 1's twelve; terminal-label vs. final-transition coherence enforced).

## Distribution
- `calls.jsonl` + `splits.json` (leave-one-campaign-out + temporal-blocking manifests) + `manifest.json` with **SHA-256** per file.
- Release gated on re-identification check: membership-inference and singularity screening over released rows; results documented here before upload.

## Maintenance
Versioned `tgv-bench/<major>.<minor>`; regenerate with a new seed rather than mutating the frozen release. Hosted on Hugging Face datasets.
