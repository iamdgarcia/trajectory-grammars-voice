# Review — Reviewer 1 (Methodology Expert: formal methods, sequential analysis, state machines)

## Summary
The paper proposes a typed state machine ("trajectory grammar") over voice-agent conversation phases with four banking terminal classes, a drift statistic (cycle depth), and a sequential stopping layer (SPRT / confidence sequences) over an outcome posterior, plus a KPI-wired evaluation protocol and intervention coupling. The positioning against the landscape in OUTLINE.md is accurate and unusually honest: TRACER is credited as nearest neighbor, the SPRT provenance (ConSol) is correctly placed at the wrong level, and §8 openly flags emission independence as the load-bearing idealization. However, the formal layer — the paper's entire claim to novelty over TRACER, namely "a guarantee" — is currently defective: the Trajectory Stopping Theorem's null-case time bound is sign-flipped and false as displayed, the cost-asymmetry proposition's stated ordering contradicts its own proof, the error-control claim for the actual multi-class rule is unproven, and the headline constant k*(δ,c) is never defined. The grammar definition itself is incomplete (no transition type reaches three of the four terminal classes) and inconsistent with both its transition table and two of its figures.

## Score
**5/10 — Major Revision.** The framing, landscape verification, and limitations sections are above the bar for this venue, and §4.4's floor-composition identity (eq:compose) is exactly the right way to avoid the "early enough to act" overclaim — it states a checkable condition rather than a guarantee. But a methods reviewer cannot accept a paper whose centerpiece theorem contains a displayed bound that is negative on the right-hand side (hence false for every positive expected decision time), whose companion proposition asserts the opposite of what its own proof derives, and whose error control is asserted by a proof sketch that does not cover the rule actually defined. These are all fixable, which is why this is a major revision rather than a reject.

## Mandatory revisions

1. **[P0] Sign error in the Trajectory Stopping Theorem's null-case time bound** (`sections/04_stopping.tex`, Theorem `thm:stopping`, eq `eq:times`, second display). As printed, E[k_dec | θ_0] ≤ (α_a A_a + (1−α_a) B_a)/|μ_0^a|. With the standard Wald boundaries of eq:boundaries, A_a = log((1−β_a)/α_a) > 0 and B_a = log(β_a/(1−α_a)) < 0 whenever α_a + β_a < 1, so the numerator α_a A_a + (1−α_a) B_a is negative (e.g., α=β=0.05 gives 0.05·2.944 − 0.95·2.944 ≈ −2.65) while |μ_0^a| > 0 — the theorem asserts a negative upper bound on a positive quantity. The classical approximation is E_0[N] ≈ [(1−α_a)B_a + α_a A_a]/μ_0^a with μ_0^a < 0 (negative divided by negative), i.e., the displayed formula must be negated, or |μ_0^a| replaced by μ_0^a. Fix the display and re-verify the θ_a-case numerator sign while at it (that one is positive and fine).

2. **[P0] Error control for the rule of Definition `def:rule` is not established by the proof sketch** (`sections/04_stopping.tex`, §4.2–4.3). The classical Wald bounds P(declare θ_a | θ_0) ≤ α_a hold for a two-sided SPRT that stops at the first crossing of *either* boundary of the monitored ratio. Definition `def:rule` declares θ_0 only when **all** class-wise ratios are ≤ their B_a; the class-a ratio is therefore *not stopped* at its own lower boundary and may continue past it (while another class's ratio is still above its B), after which it can re-cross A_a. The relevant error event is the ever-crossing probability P_0(∃k: Λ_k^a ≥ A_a) of a negatively drifted walk, which the cited two-sided SPRT machinery does not bound by α_a. Either (a) add the missing assumptions that make it true — correctly specified emission densities so that e^{Λ^a} is a θ_0-supermartingale, then Ville: P_0(∃k: Λ_k^a ≥ A_a) ≤ e^{−A_a} = α_a/(1−β_a) ≤ α_a — and write that proof; or (b) redefine the rule so each per-class test stops at its own boundaries and redo the θ_0-acceptance semantics. Also add to the theorem's hypothesis list what is currently only implied: (iii) correct specification of the emission model, (iv) almost-sure termination of the grammar walk or E[k_term] < ∞ (note `tool-call` is a q_tool→q_tool self-loop, so unbounded runs are trivially representable — the sketch's "the walk terminates almost surely" is currently an unstated assumption, not a fact), and (v) integrability/boundedness of increments for Wald's equation. Finally, the sketch's "optional stopping ... preserves the martingale property" is garbled; optional stopping does not preserve the martingale property, it bounds stopped expectations under conditions.
