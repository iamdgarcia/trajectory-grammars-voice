# Review — Novelty/Positioning (r8, senior area chair)

## Summary
The paper claims a two-part delta over the nearest neighbor TRACER: (i) a formal trajectory grammar for voice-agent conversations with KPI-aligned terminal classes and a cycle-depth drift statistic, and (ii) a sequential stopping rule with a bounded expected decision time, with TRACER's threshold shown to be its fixed-sample instance. Positioning is the paper's strongest dimension: §2 gives a five-strand taxonomy, each strand closed with an explicit "delta" paragraph, and the TRACER summary (§2.5) is specific, generous, and accurate. The delta is real but rests on two overbroad absence claims and a contestable characterization of the POMDP lineage; the delta is also anchored to three self-published (Zenodo, unreviewed) "floors" papers.

## Score (1-10)
**7.** As a positioning exercise this is above the bar: the intersection claim ("this intersection, not any single strand, is the contribution space", §2.7) is the right defensive structure, no literal "first" appears, and the TRACER-as-fixed-sample-instance framing converts a competitor into a special case without disparagement. The multi-class outcome space, voice-physics emission channels (endpoint errors, barge-ins, tool-fail), and intervention latency accounting are genuine, nameable deltas. What holds it back: the contribution is conceptual/protocol-only with all empirics deferred to a promised HF benchmark; the "guarantee" narrative leans on theorem conditions I cannot verify from the assigned files; and two universal "no work exists" claims are falsifiable by the classical state-machine dialogue-management literature and mid-call speech analytics, respectively. Survivable at a solid venue after a revision pass that scopes the absence claims.

## Mandatory revisions
1. **Overbroad absence claim about formal models** — §1 ("No formal model connects the partial transcript of an in-flight voice conversation to the terminal outcome") and §2.7 ("no work defines a formal state space for voice-agent conversations"). Finite-state/ATN dialogue managers and dialogue process grammars are exactly formal state spaces over dialogue phases; the honest delta is the *composition*: KPI-aligned terminal classes + typed voice/tool emissions + cycle-depth as a drift statistic. Add "to our knowledge", and either cite the FSA/ATN dialogue-management lineage in §2 or restate the claim at the composition level in both places.
2. **Unfair POMDP scoping** — §2.4: "The POMDP lineage also carries no decision-time guarantee … its policy optimizes long-run reward, not when a decision may be safely taken." Optimal stopping is a classical POMDP problem class; the absence is in the *dialogue-management instantiation*, not the lineage. Rewrite: the dialogue-management instantiation optimizes long-run reward and does not bound when acting on the belief is justified; this paper imports that stopping machinery explicitly.
3. **Scope the "guarantee" language** — Abstract ("achieves expected decision time at most K(ε,c) at confidence 1−ε"), §1 contribution 2, §9 ("supplies what prior early-warning systems lack"). Each occurrence should carry, in one clause, that the bound holds conditional on a calibrated/valid confidence sequence over the outcome posterior; otherwise "guarantee" overclaims relative to what any deployment can assert on production audio.

## Optional suggestions
1. §2.4: one sentence crediting the anytime-valid confidence-sequence literature so the stopping rule's provenance is explicit rather than implied-novel.
2. Positioning leans on three unreviewed Zenodo preprints cited ~6 times across §1–§2; state their publication status once so referees don't discover it themselves.
3. A compact delta table (strand × what-it-lacks × what-this-paper-adds) would compress the five per-strand "delta" paragraphs and help an AC skimming.
4. §9 "the missing control surface": supported by the taxonomy, but "missing" is rhetorical; "absent from prior formalizations" is defensible without debate.

## Questions for authors
1. Is the Trajectory Stopping Theorem's K(ε,c) bound conditional on posterior calibration, and what does miscalibration on real audio (endpointing-error channel, §2.5's warning) do to the guarantee?
2. Beyond typed emissions and KPI-terminal classes, what in the grammar is not already expressible as a classical state-machine dialogue manager — is cycle depth the entire conceptual increment?
3. Will the promised synthetic HF benchmark exist at submission, or should the paper be positioned explicitly as a formal-plus-protocol contribution?

Most attackable single claim: §2.4's "The POMDP lineage also carries no decision-time guarantee" — a factual claim about a large literature that one optimal-stopping POMDP citation falsifies.
