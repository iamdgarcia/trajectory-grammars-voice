# Review — r3_voice_agent_domain (Voice-Agent Engineering Domain Expert)

## Summary
The paper models banking voice-agent conversations as a typed grammar over seven progress states and four telephony KPI terminals, with cycle depth as a drift statistic, a multi-channel observation model explicitly designed around the near-chance Spanish text-only result, and three graph-level interventions (escalate, clarify, fraud-flag) gated by the published latency and cessation floors. The domain grounding is unusually strong: emitters are named production components, channel events (barge-in) deliberately do not mutate state, and the TTS-queue/cessation composition for mid-utterance takeover is realistic. The main defects are an internal contradiction in the fraud signature (it uses `user-answer` transitions to grow cycle depth, but `user-answer` is typed progress and cannot increment D), a clarify-intervention that resets the very drift evidence that triggered it, and an overclaim that auth-failure cycling is "the" social-engineering signature.

## Score: 7/10
This is the rare formal paper whose state set a voice engineer could actually wire into a live cascade: the terminal classes are real KPIs with operational definitions, the audit-overlay treatment of compliance escape matches post-hoc QA practice, and the separation of channel events from conversation state is correct and often gotten wrong. The multi-channel observation model is the right engineering answer to the published 0.40–0.54 text-only result. But three things keep it out of accept range: the fraud-signature mechanism contradicts the paper's own Definition 4; the clarify intervention creates a policy-controlled reset loop on D with no intervention budget; and the fraud section's coverage claim ignores passed-authentication account takeover, the dominant modern pattern.

## Mandatory revisions
1. **§6.3 (fraud signature) vs. Table 1 / Definition 4 (§3.2):** The signature says "D(σ) growing across tool-fail **and user-answer** transitions." Table 1 types `user-answer` as *progress*, and Definition 4 counts only regression types (`tool-fail`, `endpoint-error`, re-opening `agent-clarify`) toward D. As written the mechanism cannot fire. Fix: either add an `auth-fail` regression type to Σ/Table 1 or restate the signature in terms of q_auth revisit counts.
2. **§6.1 clarify action:** "resetting the drift statistic D(σ) for the current cycle" erases the evidence the posterior just used and permits unbounded monitor-initiated clarify loops (each new token resets D to zero and hysteresis does not block same-action repeats). Specify a per-call intervention budget and restrict D resets to *user-confirmed* resolution, as §3.2 already hints.
3. **§6.3 overclaim:** Repeated auth failure is at least as often benign (forgotten credentials, undelivered SMS OTP, elderly users) and the pattern is blind to passed-auth ATO/SIM-swap fraud, which walks the normal spine. State explicitly that walk-shape fraud detection covers the credential-failure subset and is a complementary layer, not "the" fraud monitor.

## Optional suggestions
1. Multi-intent calls (transfer + report lost card) re-enter `q_intent` legitimately and will inflate D; discuss discrimination or a multi-goal spine.
2. Teardown race: `service-complete` and `hangup` share the "telephony teardown" emitter; specify event ordering/tie-break for the classic "caller says thanks and hangs up mid-close" containment/abandonment ambiguity.
3. `endpoint-error` sources are restricted to q_intent/q_confirm; in production endpointer errors fire at any turn, and confirm→intent re-capture is coarser than reality (re-prompt the current turn).
4. `endpoint-error` and `barge-in` in f_k are causally coupled (late endpointer → overlap → barge-in), straining the conditional-independence assumption (i) of Theorem 1; flag it where the voice-physics channels are introduced (§3.3).

## Questions for authors
1. Is the s_k turn-state channel itself validated on hard cuts — the exact regime where the cited text-only pipeline is near chance?
2. What benign base rate of auth-failure cycling (OTP non-delivery, forgotten credentials) did you assume, and what FPR does the first-credible-signal fraud trigger imply for step-up auth volume?
3. Is t_escalation-only in eq. (14) defined and measured anywhere, or a modeling construct?
