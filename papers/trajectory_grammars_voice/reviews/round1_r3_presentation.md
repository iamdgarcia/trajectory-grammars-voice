# Review — Reviewer 3 (Writing and Presentation)

## Summary
This is a genuinely well-written paper: the prose is disciplined, the four-strand background is fair to prior work, §8's honesty about scope (framework + protocol, not leaderboard results) is exemplary, and the one-sentence-per-line convention is respected throughout. The abstract is an appropriate ~230 words and the paper reads as one coherent argument, not nine stapled sections — §3 explicitly hands off to §4–6 ("stops on it, measures it, acts on it"). The problems are all at the seams between the formal layer and the applied layer: notation defined in §3 drifts or collides by §4–6, three of the four TikZ figures contradict their own captions or the definitions they illustrate, Table 1 overflows the text block, Proposition 2's inequalities contradict its own proof sketch and surrounding prose, and the abstract and conclusion overclaim relative to §5.7/§8. Every finding below is a small, localizable fix; none requires restructuring.

## Score
**6 / 10.** As a piece of academic writing this is above the bar — clear voice, honest limitations, well-targeted related work — but a careful reader cannot currently trust the paper's own cross-references: two different quantities are both called $k^*$ (one page apart in §4), the posterior is defined over a 4-channel observation in §3 and then used as transcript-only $p(\theta \mid x_{1:k})$ everywhere else (including in the same sentence that demands multi-channel conditioning), the cost function $c$ is "recalled from Section 3" where it was never defined, and Figs. 1–3 each contradict the text or themselves. These are presentation-level defects, but they are contradictions, not style preferences, so the paper is not acceptable until they are fixed.

## Mandatory revisions
1. **Resolve the $k^\ast$ symbol collision.** `sections/03_grammar.tex` (Lemma `lem:cyclebound`, Remark `rem:drift_trigger`) defines $k^\ast = \max\{k : \Lambda_k \leq \tau_{resp}\}$ — a per-turn tool-loop bound — while `sections/04_stopping.tex` (Theorem `thm:stopping`, following §4.2) defines $k^\ast(\delta, c)$ as the expected-decision-time bound; §4.4 then uses the Lemma-2 meaning ("a run exceeding $k^\ast$ tool-loop cycles") two paragraphs after the theorem. The abstract, intro contribution 2, `figures/fig_eval_stack.tex` ("decide at $k_{\mathrm{dec}}\leq k^\ast$"), and §7 ("the stopping constants $k^\ast$ and $\delta$") all ride the ambiguity. Rename the cycle bound (e.g., $k_{\mathrm{cyc}}$) everywhere.
2. **Resolve the $\delta$ symbol collision.** `sections/03_grammar.tex` Def. 1: $\delta : Q \times \Sigma \rightharpoonup Q$ is the transition function; `sections/04_stopping.tex` Thm. 1 and §5.3 use $\delta$ as the error budget ($1-\delta$ confidence). Rename one (transition function → $\Delta$, or error budget → $\epsilon$-family).
3. **Resolve the $\Lambda$ symbol collision.** `03_grammar.tex` Lemma 2: $\Lambda_k$ = $k$-step silence gap; `04_stopping.tex` Eq. (1): $\Lambda_k^{a}$ = log-likelihood ratio. Same base symbol, same subscript style, unrelated objects. Rename the LLR (e.g., $\ell_k^a$) or the gap.
4. **Unify the terminal-set symbol and class naming.** §3 Def. 1 uses $T=\{\theta_{cont},\theta_{aban},\theta_{hand},\theta_{esc}\}$; §1, `05_evaluation.tex` (opening line and Eq. 5) and `06_intervention.tex` use $\mathcal{T}$ with spelled-out class names; §5.3's $\mathrm{EDS}_i=(T_i - t_i^*)/T_i$ reuses $T_i$ as dialogue *length*. Pick one symbol for the outcome set, keep $\theta$-subscripts consistent (or drop them everywhere), and rename the EDS length (e.g., $L_i$).
5. **Fix the posterior conditioning contradiction.** `03_grammar.tex` Def. `def:observation`/`def:posterior` defines the posterior over the 4-channel tuple $o_{1:k}$ (transcript, turn states, tool events, acoustic flags); §4.1–§4.2, §5, §6, §7, and `fig_eval_stack.tex` all write $p(\theta \mid x_{1:k})$ — transcript-only. `04_stopping.tex` §4.1 even states "the posterior must condition on the multi-channel emission stream, not on the transcript alone" in the same paragraph where Def. `def:rule` Eq. (1) conditions on $x_{1:k}$ alone, and its emission list (5 types) omits the turn-state channel that Def. `def:observation` includes. Replace $x_{1:k}$ with $o_{1:k}$ (or an emissions sequence $e_{1:k}$) throughout §4–7 and the figures.
6. **Define the cost function $c$ — the back-reference is dangling.** `04_stopping.tex` §4.5 opens "Recall the banking cost $c(\hat{\theta},\theta)$ … from Section~\ref{sec:grammar}", but §3 never defines $c$; it is used in Thm. 1 ($k^\ast(\delta,c)$), Prop. 2, Eq. (5) of §5, and §6.4. Add a definition (even symbolic, with the ordering axioms Prop. 2 assumes) in §3 or as a numbered definition at the start of §4.5. While sweeping, define or cite at first use: $\lambda_{\mathrm{mon}}^{\mathrm{irr}}$ (§5.4), $t_{\mathrm{escalation\text{-}only}}$ (Eq. 4, also in `fig_stopping.tex` caption), $\pi_\theta$ (Eq. 5), and $\tau_{\mathrm{human}}$ (§5.4 — currently only implicitly carried by the citation).
7. **Make Theorem 1's confidence claim precise.** Thm. 1 concludes "$\mathbb{E}[k_{\mathrm{dec}}] \leq k^\ast(\delta,c)$ at confidence at least $1-\delta$, where $\delta$ bounds the total error budget across classes" — but $\delta$ is never tied to the per-class $(\alpha_a,\beta_a)$ (union bound? $\delta = \sum_a (\alpha_a+\beta_a)$?), and the theorem controls only $\Pr[\text{declare }\theta_0\mid\theta_a]$ and $\Pr[\text{declare }\theta_a\mid\theta_0]$: there is no bound on action-class confusion $\Pr[\text{declare }\theta_b \mid \theta_a]$, $b\neq a$, under which the headline "confidence $1-\delta$" is ambiguous. State the map $\delta \leftrightarrow \{(\alpha_a,\beta_a)\}_a$ explicitly and either bound or scope out cross-class confusion. (The honesty of §8 about independence is good and should stay.)
8. **Fix Proposition 2's direction — it contradicts its own proof.** `04_stopping.tex` Prop. `prop:asymmetric` asserts $\alpha_{\textit{compliance\_escape}} < \alpha_{\textit{handoff}} \ll \beta_{\textit{compliance\_escape}}$ and $A_{\textit{compliance\_escape}} > A_{\textit{handoff}}$ (compliance escape fires *last*, at the highest bar). Its own proof sketch derives the opposite — unbounded $c(\theta_0,\textit{compliance\_escape})$ drives the firing threshold toward zero, i.e., fire *early* ($A$ small, $\alpha$ relatively large, $\beta$ small) — and the surrounding prose agrees with the proof: §4.5 "fire its compliance escape at the first credible signal"; §6.4 "the first confident posterior on escape triggers intervention regardless of queue state". (Also, "drives that ratio's complement toward zero" is garbled — the ratio goes to zero, its complement to one.) Flip the proposition's inequalities (or the cost story) and reconcile all three passages; note the current $\beta_{\textit{compliance\_escape}} \gg$ reading also prices the *unbounded-cost miss event as the loose one*, which is economically indefensible.
9. **Rewrite `figures/fig_grammar.tex` to match §3 and Table 1.** (a) Only six progress states are drawn (gr, au, in, tl, cf, cl) — $q_{clarify}$ is missing, yet Prop. 1's canonical cycle $q_{confirm}\!\to\!q_{clarify}\!\to\!q_{confirm}$ and Table 1's `agent-clarify` row depend on it; (b) the caption claims the arrows "carry the eight event classes of Table~\ref{tab:transitions}" but the edge labels (answer, verify, request, result, confirm, close, re-auth, regress, no-confirm, defer, policy breach) are not those types — only `tool-fail` matches; (c) the terminal wiring contradicts §3's operational definitions: §3 says $\theta_{hand}$ fires from *any* $q_p$ and $\theta_{aban}$ from *any* pre-terminal disconnection, but the figure routes both from the confirm state only, and $\theta_{esc}$ (defined as terminal confirmation *without* the guarded `disclosure` edge) is drawn as "policy breach" out of $q_{tool}$; the `disclosure` transition itself is absent; (d) the self-loop `\draw (au) edge[bend right=35, looseness=1.4] … (au);` (line 31) is degenerate — `main.log` records the pgf warning "Returning node center instead of a point on node border … node ``au''" twice; use `edge [loop below]`. Use the two-char state labels with a legend, or spell the states out — "cf" vs "cl" is unreadable as printed.
10. **Make `figures/fig_cycles.tex` agree with its own caption.** The caption (in `03_grammar.tex`) says "Two runs of equal length … Length is identical", but panel (a) draws 10 turn dots, panel (b) draws 13, and panel (b)'s text says "same turn count as 1.4$\times$ a contained call" — contradicting both the caption and Prop. 1's equal-length construction. Redraw with equal counts and delete the 1.4× sentence. The panel also invents a drift-warning threshold "$D\geq 2$" that appears nowhere in the text (Remark 1's alarm is the $(k^\ast{+}1)$-th tool-call event); align or remove.
11. **Make `figures/fig_stopping.tex` depict the rule the text defines.** It plots "posterior" curves $P(\textit{contained}\mid x_{1:k})$ and $P(\textit{handoff}\mid x_{1:k})$ rising monotonically and crossing fixed dashed lines labelled "accept $H_1$ ($\geq 1-\delta$)" / "accept $H_0$". Three problems: SPRT boundaries are crossed by accumulated log-ratios $\Lambda_k^a$ (Eq. 1–2), not posteriors; two class "probabilities" both rise above 1 simultaneously, which is impossible for a normalized posterior; and the $H_0/H_1$ vocabulary is never used in the text (the paper's classes are $\theta_0/\theta_a$), while "≥ 1−δ" conflates boundary height with confidence. Redraw as accumulating $\ell_k^a$ vs $A_a/B_a$ and relabel.
12. **Fix the Table 1 overfull box.** `main.log`: "Overfull \hbox (45.50638pt too wide) in paragraph at lines 71--85" — exactly the `tabular` body of `03_grammar.tex` (lines 71–85); Table 1 runs ~1.6 cm into the margin. Use `\footnotesize`, drop the "Class" column into the Direction column, or abbreviate the Source→Target cell contents.
13. **Align the conclusion's release claim with §5.7.** §5.7 promises only "a frozen, schema-identical *synthetic* Spanish benchmark" publicly; `09_conclusion.tex` says "The Spanish trajectory benchmark and the frozen evaluation splits will be published on Hugging Face" — which reads as releasing the real corpus. Insert "synthetic" (and say whether the frozen splits are synthetic-only).
14. **Soften the abstract to match §8.** Abstract: "a sequential stopping rule … that *achieves* expected decision time at most $k^\ast$ turns at confidence $1-\delta$" and "we *show* that a recent learned early-failure detector is the fixed-sample instance of this rule". §8 concedes the independence idealization is load-bearing and unverified for banking trajectories, and Corollary 1 is an interpretive identification, not a demonstrated result. Add the qualifier "under standard SPRT assumptions" (mirroring intro contribution 2's framing) and change "show" to "cast"/"argue". Same treatment for "thereby becomes the missing conversation-scale control layer".
